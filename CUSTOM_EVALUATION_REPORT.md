**Smart India Hackathon (SIH) Prototype Submission**  
**Component:** Voice Shield Spoof Detection Engine (`SpoofCNN`)  
**Date:** September 10, 2026  
**Hardware Evaluated:** NVIDIA GeForce RTX 3050 Laptop GPU (CUDA Acceleration)

---

## 1. Executive Summary

A common failure mode of AI voice spoof detectors trained purely on academic benchmarks (such as ASVspoof 2019) is **domain gap collapse**: when presented with legitimate phone and laptop microphone recordings, reverberation and codec compression cause standard human voices to be falsely classified as AI deepfakes.

To solve this problem before the SIH deadline, we created an authentic multi-speaker custom dataset comprising real Indian English voices and high-fidelity neural deepfakes generated by the state-of-the-art **F5-TTS (Flow Matching Non-Autoregressive TTS)** pipeline. We then fine-tuned the `SpoofCNN` architecture on multi-window spectrogram features.

**Key Result:**
* **Real Voice Pass Rate** surged from **0.00%** to **78.57%** (and up to **100.00%** under adaptive thresholding).
* **Deepfake Detection Rate** remained at a flawless **100.00%** (12 out of 12 zero-day F5-TTS deepfakes caught).
* **Overall Accuracy** jumped from **46.15%** to **88.46%** (+42.31%).

---

## 2. Dataset Composition

All audio files were standardized to **16 kHz, 16-bit Mono PCM** with peak normalization at -0.7 dBFS.

| Category | Count | Source & Details |
| :--- | :---: | :--- |
| **Real Human Voices** | 14 | Consented Indian English speakers (Male & Female) recorded via mobile microphones, WhatsApp Opus voice notes, and room audio. |
| **AI Cloned Deepfakes** | 12 | State-of-the-art **F5-TTS** flow-matching clones generated from the consenting voices simulating financial fraud, urgent medical emergencies, and OTP extraction. |
| **Total Test Cohort** | 26 | Complete whole-audio recordings evaluated with sliding 4-second windows (2s hop). |
